If you've found Or Hakerem through Airbnb or Booking.com, you've likely already seen the price. Here's something worth knowing: **booking directly with us is up to 15% cheaper** for the same apartment, the same dates, and the same level of service.

This isn't a promotional claim — it's a direct consequence of how third-party platforms work. This post explains the mechanics, what you actually get when you book direct, and how to do it.

## Why Third-Party Platforms Cost More

When you book through Airbnb or Booking.com, two additional cost layers appear:

**Platform service fees** added to the guest price — Airbnb's guest service fee currently ranges from 0% to 16.5% depending on the reservation. For a five-night stay at a luxury penthouse, that fee alone can amount to several hundred euros.

**Host fees** charged to the property owner — platforms take a percentage from the host side as well. This is a cost that gets factored into the base price you see.

The result: the same apartment, booked through a platform, typically costs 10–20% more than booking directly with the owner — even before you factor in platform-specific restrictions on payment, communication, and flexibility.

## What You Actually Pay For

When you book through a platform, you are paying for:
- Discoverability (the platform brought you to the listing)
- A review system
- A rebooking guarantee in edge cases
- Payment processing

Once you've already found the property — which you have, since you're reading this — you no longer need the platform for discoverability. The question becomes: is the review guarantee and payment processing worth a 10–15% premium?

For most guests, once they have a direct relationship with the host, the answer is no.

## What Direct Booking Gives You

**Lower price.** Direct bookings at Or Hakerem are up to 15% cheaper than the equivalent stay on Airbnb or Booking.com. The saving is real and straightforward.

**Direct communication.** When you book direct, you communicate with us — not through a platform messaging system. This means faster responses, clearer answers to questions about the apartment, and better coordination on arrival time, special requests, and Shabbat setup.

**Flexible payment options.** Platform bookings generally require payment through the platform's system. Direct bookings can be arranged via bank transfer, Bit, or cash — whichever suits you.

**More flexibility on dates and requests.** Platform terms can be rigid. Direct booking allows us to discuss your specific situation: minimum stays, early check-in, late check-out, mid-stay cleaning, and other arrangements that platforms don't accommodate well.

**Shabbat and kosher setup coordination.** If you need Shabbat keys, a hot plate, timers, or kosher kitchen preparation, this is much easier to coordinate directly. Platform messaging is slow and limits what we can communicate before arrival.

**Concierge services.** Our [concierge services](/services) — grocery delivery before your arrival, airport transfers, babysitting, restaurant reservations — are easiest to arrange through direct communication. We'll have your full stay picture before you arrive.

Here's what we offer in terms of trust signals:
