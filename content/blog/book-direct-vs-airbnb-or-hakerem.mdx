If you found Or Hakerem through a booking platform, you may also want to request a direct quote for the same apartment and dates. The useful comparison is not a headline percentage: it is the full total, the booking conditions and the communication process for the stay you are actually planning.

Booking through a platform and booking directly are both legitimate routes. They simply offer different experiences. This guide explains what changes between them and the questions to ask before you choose.

## What a booking platform gives you

Platforms combine discovery, messaging, payment processing, a review system and support into one product. For some travellers, especially those who prefer to keep every part of a trip inside one account, that structure is reassuring.

They can also make it easy to compare several properties at once. The limitation is that the information is often built for a standard reservation flow. If you have a more specific request — a longer visit, a group with different arrival times, a Shabbat question or uncertainty about which apartment fits — the platform interface may not answer everything.

## What a direct enquiry gives you

Direct booking starts with a conversation. You choose the property, send the actual dates and explain the practical details that matter. The team can then provide the relevant booking information for that request.

This does not mean you should skip your usual checks. It means you should make them explicit: compare the full quote for the same dates, confirm the property, understand what is included and keep the confirmation details in writing.

For a step-by-step checklist, read [How to Book a Tel Aviv Apartment Directly With Confidence](/blog/book-tel-aviv-apartment-directly-with-confidence).

## Compare the same stay, not a headline rate

Rates for short-term apartments change with dates, nights, seasonality and the apartment selected. The reliable comparison is therefore a quote for the same check-in, check-out, number of guests and property.

When you request a direct quote, make sure it states:

- the apartment being quoted;
- the check-in and check-out dates;
- the guest count;
- the full total and clearly applicable fees;
- the booking confirmation process;
- the cancellation terms or where to find them.

If you are comparing a platform listing, put the same details side by side. A price without the matching dates or conditions is not a meaningful comparison.

## Ask the questions a good host should welcome

Direct communication is most useful when it is specific. Include your arrival time, group size and any needs that could affect the stay. For example:

- Is the apartment suitable for the number of guests in my group?
- What is the process after I submit my dates?
- Which details should I confirm before arrival?
- What should I plan if my arrival is late or close to Shabbat?
- Who should I contact if a practical question comes up before the stay?

If you are planning a month or more in Tel Aviv, say so in the first message. Our [long-term and monthly stays guide](/blog/long-term-monthly-stays-tel-aviv) will help you think through the practical needs of a longer visit.

## Check independent information as well

Use more than one source when you are deciding. The official property page is the best place for the apartment details, while public reviews and map listings can help you understand how the business presents itself elsewhere.

You can view the official [Or Hakerem Google Business Profile](https://www.google.com/maps?cid=11119085925362597877) for the business location and guest feedback. Read reviews thoughtfully: a smaller number of detailed, relevant comments can be more useful than a large total with little context.

## Keep the confirmation together

Once you decide to proceed, save the essential information in one place: the confirmed address, stay dates, guest count, contact person, total, arrival process and any special arrangements. Share it with every traveller who needs it.

This is especially important if one person books while another arrives first, or if your group has different travel schedules. A direct booking should feel clearer as the trip gets closer, not less clear.

For more involved stays, use the [contact page](/contact) as well as the booking request. A short, complete message is easier to answer than several fragmented questions.
