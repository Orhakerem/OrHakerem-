organisations below have a live public contact, media, partnership, or resource
page checked on 19 July 2026. A contact page does **not** mean that the
organisation accepts guest posts or guarantees a link.

not offer payment, a reciprocal-link arrangement, a bulk listing, or an
automated submission. Keep every entry on `HOLD` until a person has reviewed
the destination site, its editorial fit, and its current outreach policy.

## Outreach and tracking rules

1. Review the target site's current editorial or partnership policy immediately
   before any contact. Remove prospects that require paid placement, directory
   submissions, link exchanges, or an advertising package.
2. Send a personal message that names the recipient's audience and offers the
   resource first. Ask whether it is useful; do not ask for a link as the
   primary favour.
3. If a link is earned, log it in the operating tracker with: site, contact,
   target article, send date, response, obtained URL, link type and anchor
   text. Use a descriptive, natural anchor chosen by the publisher.
4. Keep public-authority, immigration and community-organisation entries under
   the strictest review: their inclusion is for possible factual-resource
   collaboration only, never a commercial endorsement request.
