## How to use this register

- A **citation** improves entity consistency and local discovery. It is not counted as an editorial backlink.
- An **editorial opportunity** is a website that may choose to mention or link to a useful Or Hakerem guide. It is not a guaranteed placement.
- A **listing** is a property or event profile. A **distribution** channel may affect availability, pricing or host obligations; neither is counted as an editorial backlink.
- Never pay for ranking links, require a reciprocal link, use an automated submission service or offer free stays in exchange for a followed link.
- Record the final published URL, link attribute when visible, referral traffic and any lead in the tracker below.

## Outreach drafts

Use one draft as a starting point, then personalize it with a real observation about the recipient’s audience, page or event. Do not send the same copy to multiple recipients unchanged.

### Editorial guide pitch — targets A and B

>
> Hi [Name], I’m writing from Or Hakerem in Kerem HaTeimanim, a short walk from Carmel Market and the beach. We have just published a practical [neighbourhood / three-day] guide for visitors who want to explore Tel Aviv on foot. It covers [specific useful point]. If it would genuinely help your readers, you are welcome to review it for a relevant resource page or future article: [target URL]. There is no expectation of a link — we thought the guide might be useful to your audience.

If you publish planning resources for guests, we would be happy for you to review it: [target URL]. ## Monthly review

1. Count citations, listings, distribution channels and referring domains earned editorially as four separate metrics. Do not count blocked, research-only or partner-research entries as placements.
2. Verify each live listing uses the canonical NAP and website URL.
3. Check Search Console for indexed articles, impressions, clicks and referring domains.
4. Check Analytics for referral visits and direct reservation enquiries.
5. Stop pursuing any source that requests manipulated anchors, paid ranking links or an exchange designed only to pass ranking value.
